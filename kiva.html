KIVA Hotel booking platform

A mobile app for booking hotels without middlemen. A full UI/UX redesign and a new visual system: direct hotel rates worldwide, transparent terms, and every booking managed in one place.

Main product overview: mobile hotel booking flow

Context

KIVA removes middlemen from hotel booking. The product combines direct hotel rates, transparent terms, smart search, booking, secure payment and trip management into one experience.

Project

KIVA is a hotel booking product designed around direct rates and a clearer booking experience, across mobile and web.

Brief

Redesign the UI/UX and build a new visual system that stays consistent across booking, search, payment and responsive web states.

The problem

Hotel aggregators often make the final price harder to understand as fees appear late in the flow. KIVA needed an experience where price, progress and trip details stay clear from the first screen.
