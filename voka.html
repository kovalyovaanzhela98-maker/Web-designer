Context

Project

VOKA is a 3D visualisation platform for medicine and pharma. The 3D animation page is the client’s first point of contact, so it had to show the level of expertise straight away and explain how the work is set up.

Brief

Rework the page so that it presents VOKA’s 3D expertise and leads the client towards working together. In parallel — build one visual style across all of the pages.

The problem

The old page read like a generic medical website: heavy copy, weak hierarchy and very little visual proof. It was hard for a client to judge the level of 3D expertise, the ways of working, or what the service would actually cost.

The old design

A light background, a white-and-blue palette, small type and stock photography made it read as a template medical site. The 3D models were small, the CTAs got lost, and the collaboration options were never separated out.

The service is there, but the value and the expertise don’t land straight away

The new design

I shifted the focus onto 3D visuals and real work: a dark visual system, large type and a strong hierarchy. Our Work, Cost Factors and Collaboration Options answer the client’s questions and lead towards contact.

Visuals, structure and CTA all work towards the client’s decision

The “3D animation cost factors” block

Before and after

Visual hierarchy. Small type and stock photos → large type and 3D content.
Proof of expertise. Generic descriptions → real animation examples in Our Work.
Path to an enquiry. Vague terms → clear pricing and collaboration options.

Key decisions

01

3D-first presentation

3D visuals became the main visual proof, so the client can see the level of expertise immediately.

02

Real work as proof

Real animation examples moved into Our Work, replacing generic descriptions with visible proof of expertise.

03

Transparent pricing

Cost factors are explained directly on the page, so the client can understand what affects the price before making an enquiry.

04

Clear collaboration options

Three service formats are separated clearly: ready-made, customised and custom-built 3D animation.
