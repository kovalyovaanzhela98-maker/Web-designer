Context

Project

QPdates is an international, English-language dating platform. The product was built from scratch: from the landing page and sign-up through to the feed, chat, mail and profile — on web, tablet and mobile.

Brief

Build the product as a system: shared components and states, a clear way in, and sections that don’t contradict each other. Separately — a full redesign of the landing page and marketing in the same language.

The problem

The landing sold on promises: sign-up lived as a separate step, and the way dating actually worked was never shown. In dating trust has to be earned immediately — otherwise people never reach the product at all.

The old landing page

The old landing worked as a shop window: big promises, stock imagery and a button that sent you off to a separate sign-up page. There were no real profiles on it and no clear scenario.

Plenty of promises, no product and no trust

The new landing page

I built the first screen around people and action: photos, mini profile cards, lines of chat — and the sign-up form right inside the hero, in two steps. Below it: how it works and why it’s safe.

Key decisions

01

People on the first screen

Photos, mini profile cards and lines of chat gave an emotional way in instead of promises.

02

Sign-up inside the hero

The form moved to the first screen and fits into two steps — with no jump to a separate page.

03

States and validation

Every field was built with its states, errors and a password strength meter — the interface explains rather than scolds.

04

One system on every screen

From 375 to 2560 px, mobile and tablet — the same components and rules in every section of the product.
