Context

Molement removes middlemen from hotel facility outfitting. The product combines direct hotel rates, transparent terms, smart search, facility outfitting, secure payment and trip management into one experience.

Project

Molement is a hotel facility outfitting product designed around direct rates and a clearer facility outfitting experience, across mobile and web.

Brief

Redesign the UI/UX and build a new visual system that stays consistent across facility outfitting, search, payment and responsive web states.

The problem

Hotel aggregators often make the final price harder to understand as fees appear late in the flow. Molement needed an experience where price, progress and trip details stay clear from the first screen.
